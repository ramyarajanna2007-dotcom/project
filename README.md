## 📌 Project Overview
In today’s education system, students often have innovative ideas but face difficulties in execution due to:

- Lack of collaboration tools  
- Difficulty finding right teammates  
- No centralized innovation platform  
- Limited mentorship access  

This leads to many ideas never becoming real-world solutions.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution
We built an **AI-Powered Student Collaboration & Innovation Ecosystem** that:

- 🤝 Connects students based on skills & interests  
- 🧠 Uses AI to recommend teammates and projects  
- 🚀 Supports startup and innovation development  
- 👨‍🏫 Connects mentors and industry experts  
- 📊 Tracks project progress efficiently  

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- Encourage innovation among students  
- Improve inter-department collaboration  
- Enable AI-based team formation  
- Support hackathons and startup culture  
- Provide mentorship access  
- Build industry-ready students
